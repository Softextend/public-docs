= Privacy Policy

1. General information
Soft Extend Postis Integration for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central is an extension that runs exclusively within the customer's Business Central environment. All data processed by the extension is stored in the customer's Business Central database, in accordance with the policies and permissions defined by the tenant administrators.
2. Types of data processed
The extension may process data such as: names, addresses, phone numbers and contact persons of customers and shipment recipients, sales document and shipment references, invoice values and cash on delivery amounts, parcel details (type, weight, dimensions, contents), AWB numbers, shipping labels, delivery tracking history and the credentials of the customer's Postis account. Some of this data may be personal data, depending on how the extension is used by the customer.
3. Purpose of data processing
The data is processed exclusively for the proper operation of the extension: creating courier orders, sending shipments to the Postis platform, obtaining shipping cost estimates, downloading shipping labels, tracking deliveries and reporting on shipments.
4. Sharing data with third parties
The data required for shipping is sent to the Postis platform through the official Postis API, using the Postis account configured by the customer, and from Postis to the couriers that perform the delivery. The processing of this data by Postis and by the couriers is governed by the contract between the customer and Postis and by their own privacy policies. Soft Extend does not share the data with other third parties and does not collect the customer's business data for its own purposes.
5. Telemetry
The extension may send technical telemetry to Soft Extend through Azure Application Insights, as provided by Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (for example, errors, performance and usage events of the extension). This telemetry is used only to monitor and improve the extension, and it does not include the customer's business data, such as shipment recipients or document contents.
6. User rights
Under the GDPR, data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of their personal data. To exercise these rights, users should contact the system administrator of their company, which acts as the data controller for the data stored in Business Central.
7. Security measures
The data is protected by security measures such as encryption, authentication and access control, in accordance with Microsoft's policies and the policies of the tenant administrator. The communication with the Postis API is made exclusively over HTTPS, and access to the extension is controlled through Business Central permission sets.
8. Data retention period
The data is kept for as long as it is needed for the operation of the extension and in accordance with the policies of the customer's company. Uninstalling the extension does not automatically delete the data stored in the database.
9. International data transfers
When used in cloud environments, the data may be stored in Microsoft data centers outside the EU. These transfers comply with the standard contractual clauses and the data protection policies imposed by Microsoft. The data sent to Postis is stored according to the policies of Postis.
